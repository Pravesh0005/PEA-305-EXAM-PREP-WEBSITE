⚠️ Critical Traps & Pitfalls
TRAP 1: Same SP, Two Articles — Always Loss!If two articles sold at SAME selling price, one at x% profit and other at x% loss → ALWAYS a net LOSS. Loss% = (x/10)². Example: 20% each → Loss = 4%.
TRAP 2: Successive % formulaNet change = a + b + ab/100. If both are increases, ab is positive. If one decrease, use negative. Example: +20% then -10% = 20 + (-10) + (20×-10)/100 = 10 - 2 = 8% increase.
TRAP 3: Average of consecutive numbersIf terms are in AP, average = middle term. If n consecutive numbers with average A → largest = A + (n-1)/2. For 5 consecutive with avg 21 → largest = 21+2 = 23.
TRAP 4: x% of y = y% of xSo 45% of 133.33 = 133.33% of 45 = (4/3)×45 = 60. MUCH faster to swap when one value is a nice fraction!
TRAP 5: A is x% more than B ≠ B is x% less than AIf A is 20% more than B → B is 16.66% LESS than A (not 20%). Formula: B less by = x/(100+x) × 100%.
TRAP 6: Divisibility by 6 = divisible by BOTH 2 AND 3Divisible by 11: alternating sum of digits = 0 or multiple of 11. Divisible by 88 = divisible by 8 AND 11.
TRAP 7: Trailing zeros in n!Count pairs of 2 and 5. Always dominated by 5s. Formula: ⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ + ... For 50! = 10+2 = 12 zeros.
TRAP 8: Faulty Weight profit formulaProfit% = (True weight − False weight)/False weight × 100. Example: 800g instead of 1kg → Profit = 200/800 × 100 = 25%.

💰 Profit & Loss — Master Guide EVERY EXAM

BASIC FORMULAS: Profit = SP - CP (if SP > CP) Loss = CP - SP (if CP > SP) Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100 Loss% = (Loss/CP) × 100 SP = CP × (100+P%)/100 [for profit] SP = CP × (100-L%)/100 [for loss] CP = SP × 100/(100+P%) [find CP from SP and P%] CP = SP × 100/(100-L%) [find CP from SP and L%] MARKED PRICE & DISCOUNT: MP = Marked/List Price (above CP) SP = MP × (100-D%)/100 Mark-up% = (MP-CP)/CP × 100 Profit% = (1+markup/100)(1-discount/100) - 1 × 100

🌙 One-Night-Before — 20 Must-Know Points

1. ×5: halve+zero. ×25: halve twice+00. ×125: halve 3 times+000. ×9: ×10 then subtract. ×11: keep ends, sum middle.

2. Square ending in 5: n(n+1) then 25. Example: 65²=6×7=42, append 25 → 4225.

3. Same SP, x% profit AND x% loss → ALWAYS LOSS = x²/100 %. (20% each → 4% loss)

4. Successive %: net = a+b+ab/100. (+20, -10) = 20-10-2 = 8% increase.

5. x% of y = y% of x. Use to swap when one is a nice fraction. 45% of 133.33 = 133.33% of 45 = 60.

6. A is x% MORE than B → B is x/(100+x)×100% LESS than A. (A 20% more → B 16.67% less)

7. Average of consecutive/AP terms = MIDDLE TERM. 5 consec avg=21 → middle=21 → largest=23.

8. Replacement trick: new avg = old avg + (new member - old member)/n.

9. Trailing zeros in n! = ⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ + ... For 50! = 10+2 = 12 zeros.

10. Total factors of N = A^p × B^q: (p+1)(q+1). For 1200=2⁴×3¹×5² → (5)(2)(3)=30 factors.

11. Faulty weight profit = (True-False)/False × 100%. 800g instead of 1kg → 200/800 × 100 = 25%.

12. Divisibility by 11: alternate digit sum = 0 or multiple of 11. Div by 6 = div by 2 AND 3 both.

13. HCF word problem: "greatest dividing a,b,c same remainder" → HCF of differences (a-b, b-c, a-c).

14. LCM word problem: bells ringing together = LCM. Smallest leaving same r short = LCM - r.

15. EJOTY: E=5,J=10,O=15,T=20,Y=25. Front position + Back position = 27.

16. Mixed coding: common word in 2 sentences ↔ common code in those sentences.

17. Substitution: find actual property, then substitute what it's CALLED. Blood=red=yellow (in that language).

18. Buy n get k free: actual discount = k/(n+k) × 100%. Buy 5 get 3 free = 3/8 = 37.5% discount.

19. Election: winner W% → margin = (2W-100)% of total. W=65%, margin=300 → 30%=300 → total=1000.

20. Remainder cyclicity: find the pattern length, divide power by cycle, use remainder to find answer.
